Research & Academic Projects

Multi-Modal Odometry & State Estimation for Mobile Robots
State Estimation ORB_SLAM2 2D LiDAR SAM IMU Fusion

Investigating dynamic pose estimation across noisy and unstructured environments. Evaluated motor commands with IMU dead-reckoning, addressed scale ambiguities in monocular ORB_SLAM2, and currently implementing 2D LiDAR position tracking using the sam_toolbox for robust state feedback.

Gain Scheduling & State-Feedback Control for MIMO Evaporator
Control Theory MIMO Gain Scheduling State Feedback

Designed advanced multivariable control systems for a Multi-Input Multi-Output (MIMO) evaporator. Derived state-space models and synthesized Gain Scheduling alongside State-Feedback controllers to maintain asymptotic closed-loop stability across varying operational regimes.

Autonomous Line-Following & Sorting Robot (IDP)
Hardware Integration PCB Design Microcontrollers Sensor Calibration

Led the electrical engineering sub-team (team of 6) at Cambridge to build an autonomous robot. Designed and soldered custom adapter PCB interface boards, integrated PicoBot microcontrollers, and calibrated optical, ultrasonic, and magnetic sensors for low-latency reactive control.

Wireless RF Transmitter & Receiver Communications Link
RF Electronics Signal Processing Analog Circuit

Engineered and built an end-to-end analog RF transmitter and receiver system. Successfully transmitted real-time audio signals from a CD player through custom modulation/demodulation circuitry, outputting clean audio through a speaker system.
